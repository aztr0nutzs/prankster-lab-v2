# Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 2026-06-10

This is a draft and should be reviewed before publication.

## Prankstar Privacy Policy

Prankstar: Voice Meme Studio + Sound Stash is built for local sound playback, local voice meme creation, and harmless comedy.

## Information Processed On Your Device

Prankstar may process text you type into Voice Lab, Twak-Attacks, or the local bot assistant. The app may use that text to generate a local synthetic voice clip, draft a joke line, or create mock-documentary style narration.

The app stores settings such as volume, safety acknowledgment, favorites, animation preferences, bot preferences, and generated clip metadata on your device.

## Generated Audio

If you generate and save a clip, Prankstar stores the generated audio file and related metadata in app-private storage on your device. Saved generated clips can be played from the Sound Stash.

## Voice Provider Processing

Some premium voice generation features may use a backend voice generation service. When that feature is configured and you choose to generate premium narration, the text you submit may be sent to Prankstar's backend and a voice provider to create audio.

Production builds should not include voice provider API keys. Premium generation should be verified by the backend before any provider request is made.

## Purchases And Entitlements

Future Pro subscriptions, lifetime purchases, or voice credit packs may be verified through Google Play and Prankstar's backend. Purchase tokens and entitlement status may be processed to confirm access and credit balances. Payment details are handled by Google Play, not directly by Prankstar.

## Analytics And Crash Logs

Prankstar does not currently require analytics or crash reporting for the core local soundboard experience. If analytics or crash logs are added later, this policy and the Play Console Data safety form must be updated before release.

## Deleting Your Data

You can delete generated voice clips and generated clip metadata from Settings. You can also clear recent sounds, favorites, missing generated entries, and Sound Forge presets from Settings.

Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage through Android system settings removes app-private local data from the device.

## Children's Privacy

Prankstar is intended for a general audience that can understand responsible-use guidance. Do not use the app for threats, harassment, impersonation, emergency hoaxes, or illegal activity.
