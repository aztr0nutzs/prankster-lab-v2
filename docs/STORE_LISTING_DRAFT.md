## Full Description

Prankstar is a voice meme studio and prank soundboard built for harmless comedy. Browse the Sound Stash, play quick sound effects, generate synthetic voice clips, and build mock-documentary style narration for funny fictional moments.

Use Prankstar to:

- Play bundled prank sounds from the Sound Stash.
- Generate local voice meme clips with Android text-to-speech.
- Create mock-documentary narrator lines for fictional Twak-Attacks scenes.
- Save generated prank clips to your local stash.
- Use the local bot assistant to find sounds, draft jokes, and send text into Voice Lab.
- Experiment with Sound Forge for custom generated audio.

Prankstar is designed for playful, consensual comedy. Keep clips harmless, use reasonable volume, and stop immediately if someone asks.

## Feature Highlights

- Sound Stash: quick access to categorized prank sounds.
- Voice Meme Studio: generate short synthetic narration clips.
- Twak-Attacks: mock-documentary style text and narration tools for fictional comedy.
- Generated Clips: preview and save your favorite local creations.
- Bot Assistant: local command helper for searching sounds and drafting safe joke lines.
- Sound Forge: procedural audio tools for custom effects.

## Safety Positioning

Prankstar is for harmless comedy only. Do not use the app for threats, harassment, impersonation, emergency hoaxes, illegal activity, or pranks targeting strangers.
